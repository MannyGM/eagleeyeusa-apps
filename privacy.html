Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Nidaa is made by Eagle Eye USA, LLC (“we”). This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app does not collect, store or share any of your personal data.

Information we collect

None through the app. Nidaa has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not have servers that receive data from the app.

Messages from us

When you open Nidaa, it checks a small file on our website for any message from us, such as a correction or a request for feedback. The app sends nothing about you with this check. As with any web page, the company that hosts the file (GitHub) can see your phone’s internet address when it is downloaded. We do not use this to identify you.

Feedback you send us

If you email us or use “Send feedback”, you write and send the email yourself from your own email app. We receive your email address and whatever you write, and we use them to read your feedback and reply. Ask us at any time to delete your email.

Your location

If you allow it, Nidaa uses your phone’s location to find your city and calculate prayer times. This happens on your phone. Your location is not sent to us.

To show your city’s name, or when you search for a city, the app uses the location services built into your phone (Apple’s on iPhone, Google’s on Android). Those services receive the location or search text needed to answer the request and handle it under Apple’s or Google’s own privacy policies.

You can turn location off at any time and choose a city by hand.

Settings and recordings

Your settings, and any Azan recording you add, are stored only on your phone. Recordings you add are not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them.

Notifications and alarms

Prayer notifications and alarms are scheduled on your phone. No information is sent to us to deliver them.

Children

Nidaa is suitable for all ages and does not collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, we will update this page and its effective date.
