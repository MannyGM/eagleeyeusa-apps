Azan: Prayer Times & Alarm

Accurate daily prayer times and a beautiful Azan for each prayer, wherever you are.

  • Prayer times for your location, calculated on your phone, with the method and Asr rule used in your country.
  • The Azan at each prayer time, from well-known voices (Makkah, Madinah, Mishary Alafasy, Abdul Basit), each with its own Fajr Azan.
  • “Ring even on silent” for prayers you never want to miss, such as Fajr.
  • One reminder before each prayer, a monthly calendar with Hijri dates, and Arabic prayer names.
  • Add your own Azan recording.
  • Free, with no ads and no account needed.
